Windows: Snipping Tool

Open Snipping Tool, and click the video camera icon. Or press Windows + Shift + R. Then select the part of the screen that you want to record.

Mac: your face and your screen together

  1. Open QuickTime Player. Choose File → New Movie Recording. A camera window opens. Don't press its record button.
  2. Choose View → Float on Top. The camera window now stays above other windows.
  3. Make the camera window small, and move it to a corner of the screen.
  4. Press Cmd + Shift + 5, choose your microphone in Options, and record the screen.

Google Vids: how to open it

  1. Open Chrome, Firefox, or Edge on your computer. You cannot edit in Safari or on a phone.
  2. Go to vids.google.com.
  3. Sign in with your personal Gmail account. A school account may show different menus.
  4. The start page opens. Click Record (녹화) to record, or Blank video (빈 동영상) to start editing.

Google Vids: Record

Choose what to record: Camera, Camera and screen, Screen, or Audio. Then click Start (시작하기). One recording can be up to 30 minutes.

3English subtitles

If you speak English in your video, subtitles are optional. If you speak another language, you must add English subtitles.

If you speak English

Google Vids makes captions automatically for English speech. Click Captions (자막), and choose a style.

If you speak Korean (or another language)

Google Vids does not translate captions. So make an English subtitle file with an AI agent, like ChatGPT Work or Claude. The agent can listen to your video, write down the speech with the time of each sentence, and translate it. In this way, the English subtitles match the timing of your speech.

  1. Finish your video first. Then give the video file to the AI agent, and ask it like this:

    "This video is in Korean. Write down the speech with the start and end time of each sentence. Then translate it into English, and make an English subtitle file in SRT format. The times must match the speech."

  2. Check the English and the timing. If a subtitle appears too early or too late, open the SRT file in a text editor (Notepad on Windows, TextEdit on Mac), and change its start time and end time. (The format of the file is shown below.)
  3. Add the SRT file to your video. Choose one way:
    • In Google Vids: in the captions settings, add a caption track, and upload the SRT file.
    • In YouTube Studio: after you upload your video, add the SRT file in Subtitles (자막).

An SRT file is a simple text file. Each subtitle has a number, a start time and an end time, and the text:

1
00:00:00,000 --> 00:00:04,000
Hello! This is our app, StudyMate.

2
00:00:04,000 --> 00:00:09,500
It helps students find study partners in a few seconds.
Check your subtitles After you upload your video to YouTube, watch it once with the subtitles on. Check that the English subtitles appear.

4Help from an AI agent

An AI agent, like ChatGPT Work or Claude, can work with your video and audio files. Attach your files in the chat, and write what you want. The agent gives you the new file to download. Here are some things that you can ask.

Subtitles

The agent writes down the speech in your video with the time of each sentence, and makes subtitles. It can also translate them into English. See section 3.

"Make an English subtitle file in SRT format for this video. The times must match the speech."

AI narration

The agent can make an audio file from your script with an AI voice model. The models that it can use are different for each agent, so ask it first. For example, Kokoro is a free voice model that some agents can use.

"I want to make a narration from this English script. Which voice models can you use?"

"Use a natural female voice, and make an MP3 file. Read a little slowly."

Editing

The agent can join clips, cut parts, change the speed, and add a voice or music. If you give the exact times, the result is more accurate.

"Join clip1.mp4, clip2.mp4, and clip3.mp4 in this order."

"Remove the part from 1:10 to 1:25."

"The app waits for the AI from 2:40 to 2:52. Make this part four times faster."

"Add narration.mp3 as the voice, and add music.mp3 as quiet background music. Then tell me the total length of the video."

6Upload to YouTube

  1. Go to YouTube Studio (studio.youtube.com) and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Click Upload videos (동영상 업로드), and then Select files (파일 선택).
  3. In Visibility (공개 상태), choose Unlisted (일부 공개). Don't choose Private (비공개). Other people cannot watch a private video.
  4. Copy the video link, and put it on your team's Notion page.

If you make your video in Google Vids, you can also use File → Export to YouTube (파일 → YouTube로 내보내기).
